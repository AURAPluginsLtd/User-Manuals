# Do I need to remove the DEMO before installing the FULL version?

No. The FULL installer will overwrite the DEMO version in your hard drive.

With the **AURA Plugins Download Manager** ([macOS](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-mac/) / [Windows](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-pc/)) this is one click: **Sign in**, then click **Get full version** on the Editor card and **Install** the formats you use. The Download Manager replaces the DEMO and adds the license for you.
