# Does your prices include taxes / VAT?

No. Our payment processor, [FastSpring](https://fastspring.com/) is handling all of the tax / VAT calculations during the checkout process. You can view the final price in your own currency at checkout and any taxes / VAT applied.

If you have a business, you can input the VAT number during the payment
