# Why is my order payment stuck?

Most likely you haven’t accepted all of our site cookies which are mandatory to process the payment. It is perfectly safe since we don’t sell your data to anyone, ever.

To fix this, [cancel your order](https://auraplugins.com/my-account/orders/) and place a new order. If its still stuck, try a different browser.

Safari and Chrome works for sure.
