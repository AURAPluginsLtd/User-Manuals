# I think I found a BUG, how can I report it?

The easiest way is from the **AURA Plugins Download Manager** app ([macOS](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-mac/) / [Windows](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-pc/)):

1. Click **Support** at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Click **Tickets**.
3. Under **Open a ticket**, set **Support type** to **Bug Report**.
4. Choose the **Product** and enter a **Title**.
5. Fill in **Detailed explanation of bug** and click **Create ticket**.

It is very important to include all of the details that can help us solve the issue:

- Your computer specs
- Your hardware specs
- How we can reproduce the issue

The more information we get from the Bug Report, the easier it is for our team to resolve it.

When you are signed in, your report shows under **Your tickets** in **Support** → **Tickets**. Click it to read our replies and answer with **Add notes** → **Reply**.

> **NOTE:** To attach screenshots or videos, open the ticket with **View ticket in store** and reply there.

You can also report any BUGs via our Bug Report form on the website: [https://auraplugins.com/my-account/bug-report/](https://auraplugins.com/my-account/bug-report/)

Updates are not immediate, if another update process is in the works.
