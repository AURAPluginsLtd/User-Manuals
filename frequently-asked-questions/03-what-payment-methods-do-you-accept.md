# What payment methods do you accept?

Our payments are handled via [FastSpring](https://fastspring.com/) which is one of the biggest Ecommerce payment gateways used. They have several different options from PayPal to Credit Cards and even Online Banking options for some countries.

Latest addition is Crypto Currency, if that is your thing. We do offer permanent 20% discount on Crypto Payments.

Add some products to cart and see what options you have in the checkout page!
