# Is the preorder charged upfront or at release date?

Pre-orders are charged immediately. This is a great opportunity for you to save some cash before the product is released and you will get early access to the editor.

[Refunds policy](https://auraplugins.com/refunds-policy/) applies to pre-orders without exceptions.
