# How can I review your Product?

The easiest way is the **AURA Plugins Download Manager** app. Download it for [macOS](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-mac/) or [Windows](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-pc/), open it and **Sign in** with your AURA Plugins account.

1. Click **Reviews** in the left sidebar. It lists the editors you own that you haven't reviewed yet, and the yellow number shows how many are waiting for your review.
2. Click **★ Review** on the editor you want to review.
3. Choose 1 to 5 stars, write a **Title** and your **Review**, then click **Save review**.

You can also open an editor you own with **Manage** or **Install** and click **Review** under the product image. If you have already reviewed it, the button says **Edit review**, so you can change your review later.

> **NOTE:** Reviews open 5 days after your membership starts. New reviews, and edits that lower the star rating, are checked by us before they appear on the product page.

> **NOTE:** To add photos or videos to your review, do that on the product page after you log in to the website.

**Review on the website**

Navigate to [https://auraplugins.com/my-account/](https://auraplugins.com/my-account/) and login if prompted. Once logged in, you can review products you’ve purchased.

Next, go to [https://auraplugins.com/products/](https://auraplugins.com/products/) and choose the product you want to review.

Scroll down until you see Reviews tab and you can leave a review there!
