# I just purchased the Editor, where can I download the license?

You don't need to download the license yourself. The **AURA Plugins Download Manager** installs the full version and adds the license for you.

1. Download AURA Plugins Download Manager for [macOS](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-mac/) or [Windows](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-pc/), install it and open it.
2. **Sign in** with your AURA Plugins account email and password. Active memberships unlock full version install.
3. Find your Editor in **All Editors** and click **Install** (or **Get full version** if the DEMO is already installed).
4. Click **Install** for each format you want: App, AAX, Audio Unit, VST2 or VST3.
5. The Download Manager writes the license file into the Editor data folder automatically. Open the Editor and it is registered.

> **NOTE:** Not ready to sign in yet? Click **Skip, use demos** to try the DEMO versions. You can sign in later from **Login** in the sidebar.

**Manual download from the store**

If you prefer to install by hand, for example on an offline computer:

1. Login at [https://auraplugins.com/my-account/](https://auraplugins.com/my-account/)
2. Click the Downloads page link. Once you are there, select the product you are looking for and you can download the license and Editor full installers for macOS or Windows.
