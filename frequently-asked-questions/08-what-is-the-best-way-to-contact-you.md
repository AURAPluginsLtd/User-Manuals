# What is the best way to contact you?

All of our help is in one place in the **AURA Plugins Download Manager** app. Download it for [macOS](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-mac/) or [Windows](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-pc/), open it and click **Support** at the bottom of the left sidebar.

1. **Knowledge Base** → choose your Editor → read the Changelog, the User Manual and the other guides for that product.
2. **FAQ** → click **+** on a question to read the answer.
3. **Tickets** → **Your tickets** lists your recent tickets when you are signed in. Click a ticket to read the conversation, then **Add notes** → type your message → **Reply**. A closed ticket can be opened again with **Reopen ticket**.
4. **Tickets** → **Open a ticket** → pick **Support type** (Bug Report or Feature Request) → **Product** → **Title** → describe the issue → **Create ticket**.
5. **Email** → fill in **Subject** and **Message** → **Send**. Use this only if you don't use Discord or can't access the ticket portal.

> **NOTE:** Sign in to see Your tickets and to reply from the app. Guests can still open a ticket or send an email by entering their name and email.

> **NOTE:** To attach screenshots or videos, use **View ticket in store** and reply to the ticket on the website.

Fastest way to reach us is via our [Discord](https://discord.gg/TeG7ukYcVW) server. The community is amazing and you’ll get answers almost immediately! You can also open it from **Support** → **Discord** in the Download Manager.

If you want to reach us via regular email without the app, fill the form in [contact us](https://auraplugins.com/contact/) page and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
