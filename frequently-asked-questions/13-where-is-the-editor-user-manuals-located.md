# Where is the Editor User Manuals located?

The easiest way to read the manuals is the **AURA Plugins Download Manager** app ([macOS](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-mac/) / [Windows](https://downloads.auraplugins.com/download/aura-plugins-download-manager-pc/)):

1. Click **Support** at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Click **Knowledge Base** and choose your Editor.
3. Pick **User Manual**, or any other guide in the list. The **Changelog** is there as well.

To keep a PDF copy on your computer, click your Editor's button in the Download Manager (**Try now**, **Install** or **Manage**) and click **Download PDF** when a PDF manual is published for that Editor. The manual is saved in the same folder as the Editor license file.

We are writing User Manuals as we proceed with the version xx.xx.xx updates.

All manuals will be available in [https://auraplugins.com/support/](https://auraplugins.com/support/) once the product update is wrapped up. Alternatively you can find the manual download from the product downloads page.
